# Production craft guidance

Script, storyboard/images, audio and editing are vp-production references, not separate authority, gates or agents.

Read vocabulary/script for sense selection and writing; storyboard/visuals for image planning; audio for requests/WAV; editing for timeline/video. Profile/brief choose style, voice and language; references explain how to realize those choices.

Use the existing outline/purpose/action/images/beats/anchors fields, not competing scripts or truth sources. Freeze narration before coverage/anchors. Only learner_pause_seconds is a scene-tail control; intent/pronunciation_notes do not directly control emotion. Do not promise unsupported lip-sync/SFX/camera behavior.

Technical checks do not prove perception/learning. Never claim listening/viewing/quality from prompts, ASR, hashes or metadata. Auto does not call a reviewer; craft inspection does not become a new quality gate. The new direction requires real artifact trials within the development assignment.

The v3 [director guide](legacy/director-system.md) is historical. Current loaders/callers use vp-production references without reviewer calls in engine 4 auto.
