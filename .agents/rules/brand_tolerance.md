# Canonical mascot and identity tolerance

Sources: `sys/assets/characters/channel-mascot/reference-v1.png` and `character.json`. CH01 is the canonical mascot.

## 1. Frame presence and frequency
Mascot is **not required in every frame**. It only needs to appear in **1–2 key frames** per video for brand recognition.
Two allowed presence modes:
1. **Guiding (small scale):** Appears small, using gestures or expressions to direct viewer attention to the subject/focus without obstructing it.
2. **Feature (prominent / full-frame):** Occupies the primary focus of a single frame to showcase a strong emotion, internal thought, or key action.

## 2. Core identity and brand tolerance (80/20 rule)
80/20 distinguishes core identity from minor variations; it is not a score, probability or exemption from checking meaning.
- **Required core (80%):** Round white navy-outlined head, two simple solid black oval eyes, short-sleeve light-blue `#8CCFE8` shirt, stick limbs and exactly one torso. No doubled shirts, muscular real person, large anime eye whites or silent replacement of the brand standard.
- **Allowed tolerance (20%):** Subtle eyebrows/forehead lines, sweat, situational mouth shapes, slightly rounded hands/feet and simple cartoon hands for holding/pointing. Do not reject solely for these details or turn them into repair prohibitions. Minor prop/layout differences are acceptable when the learning/action function stays correct. Tolerance does not permit missing mascot when scheduled, wrong action or wrong meaning.
